**Limitations (nonblocking).**
- Core Banking posting events are published but not consumed yet.
- Only payment summary and timeline projections exist. `ops_work_queue`,
  `customer_payment_status` and the other projections belong to later phases.
- A repair entry is resolved by SQL; there is no repair API.
- Consumer lag uses the last fetched high watermark, so an idle, stopped
  projector shows as STALE rather than LAGGING.
- PENDING reads need payment-api.
- `eventing-smoke` shares the consumer group with a walkthrough projector.
- Kafka retention is 7 days. Recovery beyond that is by rebuild.

**What dependent phases can rely on.**
- Every payment event committed through `Store.Apply` is published at least
  once with a stable ID, and carries `paymentId`, `lifecycleState` and
  `executionOutcome`. New event types project into summary and timeline without
  projector changes.
- Projections deduplicate, reorder, quarantine and rebuild without replaying
  effects.
- An effectful consumer (B15) must set `eventing.Consumer.Effectful` and keep
  its own inbox.
- F02 can poll `GET /api/v1/payments/{id}?minVersion=N` after a command and show
  PENDING and STALE states.
- B04 and later phases add commands on `Store.Apply`; their events appear in
  the timeline automatically.
