Outside this phase: No domain logic duplicated in the UI, payment explorer or case pages yet.

Dependency plan: pin React/TypeScript/Vite, `react-oidc-context` with
`oidc-client-ts`, TanStack Query and `@hey-api/openapi-ts` with its Fetch and
TanStack Query generation. Pin Playwright and `@axe-core/playwright` for browser
and automated accessibility checks. Server-side policy remains authoritative.
