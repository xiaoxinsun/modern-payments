Defects found and fixed while developing the smoke:
- The projector hung on shutdown: with BlockRebalanceOnPoll the consumer must
  AllowRebalance before Close.
- With the broker down, the idempotent producer refused to fail in-flight
  records, holding the relay transaction open; fixed with
  AllowIdempotentProduceCancellation (bounded 10s delivery, duplicates
  absorbed by the inbox).
- After a crash, the default 45s group session delayed reassignment; the
  session timeout is now 10s.
- Pre-B03 events from B02 tests lacked lifecycleState and would be poison; the
  owner now upcasts from payment_versions when reading.
