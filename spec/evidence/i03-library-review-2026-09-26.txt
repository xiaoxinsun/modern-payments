2. Cross-design build-versus-adopt review

ADR 0007 and the tracker dependency adoption register record reviewed ownership
for PostgreSQL/sqlc, OpenAPI generation/validation, Kafka, Temporal, telemetry,
adapter circuit state, CSV, frontend OIDC/server-state/client generation/browser
testing and load/replay verification. The relevant future phase packets name the
selected library and the GTB policy boundary. Exact future versions remain owned
by the phase that first imports them so stale unused modules are not pre-pinned.

The I02 migration wrapper was retained deliberately. Its immutable SHA-256
history validation, advisory serialization and validation-plus-all-pending-files
transaction are tested GTB guarantees not replaced cleanly by the reviewed general
migration tool. Standard-library HTTP/config/logging remains because the local
code expresses correlation, safe-error, readiness, redaction, shutdown and
authorization policy rather than reimplementing a wire protocol.
